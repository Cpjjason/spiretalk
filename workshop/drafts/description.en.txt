Lets you talk to the eight Ancients and the Merchant. Ancient events get a Talk option, and the shop gets a Talk button in the top right.

[h2]Features[/h2]
You get three lines per visit, and what you say changes their affection (hostile / stranger / friendly / close / devoted).

After the first talk they give you their keepsake, a relic that levels up as affection goes up and becomes a vow at devoted. There is a keepsake book in the settings.

Ancients sometimes propose a deal, like reaching act 2 alive or not drinking potions this act. Accepted deals show up as relics, and keeping one gives affection and 50 gold.

Characters remember earlier runs. You can view and edit what they remember under Memories in the settings.

Up to two new events per run: the overturned cart and an Ancient resting by the road.

Ancients who like you may put one of their own relics up for sale in the shop.

When a run ends, the character with the highest affection says a few words on the results screen.

[h2]AI[/h2]
No API key is needed. Without one, characters use pre-written lines and you pick from three replies. Everything else works the same.

With a key, lines are generated by the AI, take your current run (HP, deck, relics) into account, and you can type your own. Supports OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Zhipu, SiliconFlow, Alibaba Bailian, local Ollama and more.

Free options: Google AI Studio, or Zhipu's glm-4.7-flash in mainland China. The game walks you through it on first launch.

You can switch between the two at any time with the Mode setting at the top of the settings page.

[h2]Other[/h2]

Road event paintings and keepsake icons are generated art.
